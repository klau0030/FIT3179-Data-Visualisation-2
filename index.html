Immigration - Driving Away Employment?

Comparing total arrivals to each state adjusted per 100,000 population. Use the filter to focus on a single state. Data sources: local CSVs and GeoJSON.

What to look for
States with darker hues have more arrivals per 100,000 people. The bar chart ranks states so you can compare magnitudes precisely.
Observation
Selangor and Johor show the largest total arrivals; W.P. Kuala Lumpur and W.P. Putrajaya show 0 recorded arrivals in 2024.

This dashboard compares monthly arrivals to each Malaysian state adjusted per 100,000 population and juxtaposes these with labour force indicators (participation and unemployment rates) over time. Use the state selector to focus on a single state and the Top-N control to limit the ranked list.

Purpose: help explore where arrivals are concentrated relative to population and whether patterns align with labour market outcomes.

After normalising arrivals per 100,000 population, Perlis and Labuan appear among the highest-ranked states - their per-capita arrival rates exceed those of several larger states despite much lower absolute arrival counts.

Note: Values shown are monthly arrivals per 100k population. Use the state selector to focus on a single state.
